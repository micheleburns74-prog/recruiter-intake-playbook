# First 30 days of the search

A checklist for the first month after the intake meeting. It pairs with the [hiring plan](hiring-plan-template.md) and the [pipeline math](pipeline-math.md).

## Days 1 to 2

- [ ] Send the one-page hiring plan and ask the manager to correct it
- [ ] Confirm the four commitments in writing: 48 hour feedback, standing interview slots, the panel named, the approval ceiling
- [ ] Build the interview scorecard and agree it with the panel
- [ ] Set up the tracker the manager can open without asking you

## Week 1

- [ ] Build the search strings and test the first ten results (see the [Boolean sourcing library](https://github.com/micheleburns74-prog/boolean-sourcing-library))
- [ ] Send the first 30 outreach messages
- [ ] Put the first three profiles in front of the manager within the week
- [ ] Book the recurring weekly check-in

## Week 2

- [ ] Send the remaining outreach
- [ ] Hold the first recruiter screens
- [ ] Review the manager's feedback on the first batch and adjust the search
- [ ] Compare reply and screen rates to the pipeline math assumptions

## Week 3

- [ ] Hiring manager interviews underway
- [ ] Check the pipeline against the targets: is the number of qualified candidates on track?
- [ ] If it is not, bring one specific change to the manager: widen a requirement, add a channel, or revisit the pay ceiling
- [ ] Touch every active candidate with a named next step

## Week 4

- [ ] Final-round panel confirmed with dates
- [ ] Offer conversation started with the manager: package, approval, close plan
- [ ] Backup candidates warm in case the first offer falls through
- [ ] Share a one-page status with real numbers: sent, replied, screened, interviewed

## What good looks like at day 30

The manager has seen five or more profiles, has interviewed at least two, and has given feedback within 48 hours each time. The pipeline numbers are close to the plan, or you have already brought a specific change to close the gap.
