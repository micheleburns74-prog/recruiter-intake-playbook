# Interview scorecard

Built from the hiring manager's answers in the intake, before the first candidate is screened. Every interviewer scores the same things, so the debrief compares like with like instead of impressions.

## How to build it

1. Take the must-haves from section 2 of the [hiring plan](hiring-plan-template.md). Turn each into one competency, no more than five in total.
2. Write what a strong answer looks like for each, using the hiring manager's own words.
3. Assign each competency to one interviewer so nothing is asked twice and nothing is missed.
4. Agree the rating scale and the bar for moving forward before anyone interviews.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Clear strength, specific examples, would raise the bar on the team |
| 3 | Meets the bar with solid evidence |
| 2 | Partial evidence, a gap that would need support |
| 1 | Does not meet the bar |

## Rules for the debrief

- Ratings are submitted in writing before the group discusses, so the loudest voice does not set the outcome.
- Evidence means what the candidate said or did, not how the interviewer felt.
- A single 1 on a must-have competency needs a named reason to move forward.
- The decision and the reasons are recorded, so the next search starts smarter.

## Before the first interview

Calibrate with the two profiles from the intake: score the clear yes and the borderline using this scorecard, and check that the hiring manager agrees with the ratings.
